Om, salutations to Lord Ganesha. Om, salutations to the Gurus. O Mount Kailash, you are beautifully adorned with various kinds of trees. 1. Amidst a pavilion studded with gems and decorated with diverse flowers, the Lord of the Deities conversed with the Supreme Lord. 3. Matted locks of hair serve as ornaments for that Deity; they enhance the splendor of the Lord of the Universe and cast a blue radiance. 4. She was adorned with a tiger skin, yet even that could not rival the brilliance of the sun. 5. As an integral part of the cosmic form, Nartha—wrapped in a blanket—bowed his head and exhaled the wind.
Lord Kartikeya said: "O Lord of Lords, You are the Creator of the great deities and the Controller of all dissolution. It is Your movement that animates the presiding deities within all embodied beings. You are the source of sustenance for us and for the destitute and afflicted. You are the origin of this universe and its primal cause."

She is the Goddess who accomplishes all tasks and grants a husband to all. She is capable of fulfilling every desire; She grants all wishes, liberates one from all sorrows, saves one from the fear of death, and destroys all obstacles. She is endowed with a perfect form and adorned with supreme auspiciousness; She is omniscient and omnipotent; all Her actions bear fruitful results. She is the embodiment of all knowledge, the destroyer of all things, the foundation of all existence, and the Goddess who grants liberation from sins associated with water. She is the Goddess of all happiness, the protector of all, the source of all knowledge, and the bestower of the fruits of all desires. She is the dispeller of all sorrows, the embodiment of supreme bliss, and the abode of the universe. She is the Creatrix of the three worlds—Tripura.
