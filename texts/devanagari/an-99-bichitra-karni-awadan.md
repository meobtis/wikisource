Lion! The ocean of material existence is the very source of all knowledge. Gautama, the son of Vijayaka, called out once more. While Lord Shakya (Buddha) was immersed in his divine play within the Jetavana grove, he beheld the lifeless body of an orphan alongside thirteen hundred other monks. O Destroyer, make the world lion-like; then, dispel all fear. O Refuge of the Dharma, O Gakao—worshipped by the gods! There is not much to be found in the human realm. O Destroyer, grant me the merit of 'Dorandola'.

Lord Singh (Shakyasingha) proceeded towards Jetavana Mahavihara accompanied by the monk Svalaka and members of the Sangha. The sight of Him carrying the *pindapatra* (alms bowl) was truly captivating. Those who triumph through rigorous asceticism achieve success within just sixty days. The monks did not wish to cease their chanting; indeed, even countless Bodhisattvas appeared as mere novices in His presence. He performed numerous religious rituals. The great Shakyasinha attained immense wealth—specifically, spiritual splendor. O King, there were also four other horsemen present who were engaged in various religious sects or rituals. Many Kinnaras and other living beings, all extremely fortunate, were there as well. The sons of Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshwar—who had assumed physical forms—were also present. 3 Once again, that child was killed by Mwombo, Pipari, and Bhalpi. Present at the assembly were Brahma, Vishnu, Maheshwar, Indra, Yama, Varuna, Kubera, Hakana, Gandharvas, and Ganas, along with numerous Kinnaras. ❀ Endowed with immense splendor and radiance, and adorned with garlands, she shone like a divine goddess.
